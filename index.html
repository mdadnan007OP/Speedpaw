Understand Your Internet Better

Download Speed

Measures how fast data is downloaded to your device.

Upload Speed

Measures how fast data is uploaded from your device.

Ping (Latency)

Time for a small packet to travel to the server and back.

Jitter

Variation in ping. Lower is better for stable connections.

Connection Quality

Overall quality based on speed, ping & jitter.

What is a Good Internet Speed?

Why is My Internet Slow?

  • Too many devices connected
  • Wi-Fi signal is weak
  • Background downloads or updates
  • Old router or equipment
  • ISP congestion or network issues
